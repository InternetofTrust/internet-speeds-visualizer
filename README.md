# Internet Speed Visualizer

**Watch books fly from a bookshelf to a PC at real-world internet speeds.**

A simple, tangible visualization of what different connection speeds actually *feel* like — from the painful days of 56k dial-up all the way to modern 10 Gbps fiber.

One book = 1 MB (a typical text ebook).

---

## Why this exists

If you grew up in the 90s, you remember the ritual:

1. Double-click the dial-up icon  
2. Listen to the modem scream and negotiate  
3. Wait... and wait...  
4. Finally get online, only to watch a single image load line by line  

This little app brings that feeling back — and then shows you just how absurdly fast things have become.

Switch between:

- **Dial-up (56 Kbps)** — one book takes ~2.4 minutes  
- **DSL (5 Mbps)** — ~1.6 seconds per book  
- **Cable (20–100 Mbps)**  
- **Fiber (500 Mbps – 10 Gbps)** — hundreds of books per second

You’ll see the difference not as abstract numbers, but as actual books racing (or crawling) across the screen.

---

## Features

- Realistic dial-up modem sound (the full handshake)
- Books leave the shelf, travel the wire, and disappear just before reaching the PC
- Smooth multi-lane streaming at high speeds
- Books-per-second and time-for-1000-books stats
- Fully self-contained — works offline, no tracking, no external dependencies

---

## How to use

Just open the page and hit ▶.

That’s it.

Choose different network profiles from the dropdown to compare eras.
